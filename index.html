How to Use

  1. Write your letters. Write each small letter three times between the guide lines. It takes about ten minutes, and you only do it once. Capitals and numbers can be added later for names and addresses.
  2. Pick a card. Holiday, Thank You, Birthday, Sympathy, Just Because or Letter. Type your message and watch it appear in your handwriting.
  3. Make it yours. Choose the card size, paper, pen and ink, add a photo, and sign your name by hand.
  4. Send it. Tap Send to share it as a picture, save a PDF or print it. For envelopes, open Address Envelopes, add people from Contacts, and print them one per page.

FAQ

Why write each letter three times?
Every time a letter appears, Kindly Yours picks one of your three versions and tilts and sizes it very slightly. That is what keeps it from looking like a font. Tap “Write It Again” for a different take on the same message.

Some letters are missing on my card.
Those are letters you haven’t written yet. The card lists them in red. Tap “Write Them” to add them. Until you write capitals, small letters stand in for them.

Does it do joined-up (cursive) writing?
Not yet. Letters are placed one by one, so printed handwriting looks the most natural.

Can I use someone else’s handwriting?
Yes. Tap “Add someone else’s handwriting” on the home screen and hand them your phone or iPad. Each handwriting is kept separately.

What paper should I print on?
Cards come in 5×7 and 4×6 inches, and letters in US Letter. Choose the same paper size in the print options. For envelopes, feed one at a time and choose the envelope size (A7, A2 or #10).

Writing with a finger looks thin or shaky.
Write a little larger and slower, filling the space between the lines. On iPad, Apple Pencil gives the smoothest result. You can rewrite any letter by tapping it in your handwriting page.

Is my signature used anywhere else?
No. It is only placed on the cards you choose to sign and stays on your device. Please don’t use Kindly Yours for legal documents.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Kindly Yours (“the App”) is provided by an independent developer (“we”). This policy explains how the App handles information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We have no servers, and nothing you write or create is sent to us.

Information Stored on Your Device

Handwriting and signatures. The letters and signatures you write are stored only on your device.

Cards and envelopes. Your messages, card settings, return address and the addresses you add are stored only on your device.

Contacts. When you choose people with the system contact picker, the App receives only the names and postal addresses you pick. It cannot see the rest of your contacts.

Photos. When you add a photo to a card, the App receives only the photo you choose and keeps a copy with that card on your device.

Sharing and printing. Cards and envelopes leave your device only when you share, save or print them yourself.

Purchase

The App is purchased through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we do not receive your name, email address or payment information.

Children’s Privacy

We do not collect, store or share any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting Your Data

You can erase a handwriting or delete cards and addresses in the App at any time. Deleting the App removes everything it stored.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
